03

Know the assumptions

Each day starts with arrivals, then demand and sales. At day's end, stock plus outstanding orders is compared with the reorder point. At or below the point, one fixed-size order is placed. An order placed on day 1 with a two-day lead time arrives at the start of day 3.

Every integer between minimum and maximum demand is equally likely. The seed makes a run repeatable. Uniform demand is a teaching assumption, not a forecast. This model omits seasonality, supplier delays, costs and optimization. Zero demand has no fill rate.
